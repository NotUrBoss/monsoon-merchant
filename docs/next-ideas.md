# Ideas for after 2.3.1

These come from the 2.3.1 end-to-end GUI sweep: 43 views on 10 screen sizes, from a 320 px phone to a 1920 px desktop. They are grouped by effort, with the quickest wins first.

## Quick wins

1. **Sell everything here.** Add one button on the Market tab that sells all cargo at this port. Show the total and the profit before you confirm.
2. **Undo the last trade.** Allow an undo while you are still in port on the same day, at the exact price you paid or received. It guards against misclicks on desktop.
3. **Shorter tips on small phones.** At 320–375 px, a first-time tip fills half the screen. A one-line tip that expands when tapped would leave room for the list.
4. **"What's new" card.** Show a short card after an update, so players see what changed. Updates now install on their own from the title screen.
5. **Share a Daily result.** Copy a line such as "Daily 28 Oct · RM 3.2M · Towkay" to paste into chat. This adds a friendly competition with no server.

## Medium

6. **Desktop layout.** On screens 1000 px and wider, the game is still a 560 px phone column with empty space on both sides. A two-column layout would use that space:
   - Market and map side by side.
   - Sheets open as a side panel instead of from the bottom.
   - Keyboard shortcuts: B buy, S sell, W wait, 1–5 for tabs.
7. **Best trade from here.** Add a card on the Market tab that ranks the best known trades from this port. It would use only prices you have seen, show how old each price is, and include voyage cost and days.
8. **Price map.** Pick a good to colour each port on the chart by its last known price.
9. **Backup to a file.** Download and import a backup file, alongside the copy-paste code.
10. **"Pays back in N days" estimates** for each port when building a warehouse, factory, estate or shop. The reviewers asked for this.

## Bigger

11. **Endless charters.** Each year you choose 3 of 6 long goals. Examples are "Serve all 13 ports", "RM 2M of tyres" and "A debt-free year".
12. **Smarter redraws.** Update only the parts of the screen that changed instead of redrawing the whole screen after every action. This would make every action smoother on older phones.
13. **Accessibility settings.** Add larger text, a high-contrast mode, and badges that use shapes as well as colour.
14. **Quality surveys and the China–Vietnam overland corridor.** Both are carried over from the 2.3.0 review.
