# Ideas for after 2.4.0

## Shipped in 2.4.0

All 14 ideas from the 2.3.1 sweep, plus four game layers:

- **Captain XP and perks**: 30 levels and 13 perks; a perk choice at every level.
- **Quests**: a ten-step guild story, then odd jobs that scale with your house.
- **The Cabinet**: port stamps, cargo cards, rare cards and ship trophies, kept across runs; five sets pay Legacy.
- **Haggling**: a stop-the-marker mini-game on lots of RM 20k or more.
- The 14 ideas:
  - Sell everything here.
  - Undo the last trade. It shows in the HUD for 12 seconds, so nothing on the page moves.
  - Short tips on phones under 480 px.
  - A What's new card.
  - Share a Daily result.
  - A wide layout with a side panel, sheets docked right and keyboard shortcuts (1–5, W, A, U, C, Esc).
  - Best known trades.
  - A price map.
  - A backup file.
  - Payback estimates.
  - Endless charters.
  - Smarter redraws: the screen is patched in place and the map canvases are kept.
  - Accessibility settings.
  - Quality surveys and the Haiphong–Shenzhen truck corridor.

The 2.4.0 sweep covered 58 views on 10 screen sizes with no layout issues. All 15 browser journeys pass, including a new 2.4 journey, and there are 144 engine tests.

## Next

1. **Perk respec**: once per season, swap one perk for a fee. This helps players who picked early perks they no longer need.
2. **Quest log**: list the finished story jobs with their lines, so the chain reads like a story.
3. **Cabinet sharing**: copy a short line such as "Cabinet 31/38, Ocean Passport complete".
4. **Haggle personalities**: some traders have a faster marker or a wider zone. Show them on the port sheet.
5. **Keep off-spec tonnes flagged** when they go into a warehouse. Today the flag is lost in storage.
6. **Charter history**: list past years' charters in the Endless Hall of Houses.
7. **Buy and sell keys** on desktop: B and S open the trade sheet for the row under focus.
